# Northstar Analytics, Inc.
## Employee Handbook

**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Status:** Active

This handbook summarizes employment policies for Northstar Analytics, Inc. (“Northstar”). When a benefit plan document or Summary Plan Description conflicts with this handbook, the applicable plan document controls.

## 1. Employment Classifications

Northstar recognizes the following employment classifications:

**Full-Time Employees:** Employees regularly scheduled to work at least 30 hours per week.

**Part-Time Employees:** Employees regularly scheduled to work fewer than 30 hours per week.

**Temporary Employees:** Employees hired for a defined period or project.

**Independent Contractors:** Individuals providing services under an independent contractor agreement. Contractors are not Northstar employees and are not eligible for employee benefits unless specifically stated in a written agreement.

## 2. Paid Time Off

Northstar provides Paid Time Off (PTO) to eligible full-time employees.

Annual PTO entitlement is based on completed years of continuous service as of January 1 of each calendar year:

| Completed Service | Annual PTO |
|---|---:|
| Less than 3 years | 15 days |
| 3 through 5 years | 20 days |
| 6 or more years | 25 days |

New full-time employees receive prorated PTO during their first calendar year based on their hire date.

Part-time, temporary, and independent contractor workers do not receive PTO under this policy.

Employees may carry over a maximum of **five unused PTO days** from one calendar year into the next. PTO above the five-day carryover limit is forfeited unless otherwise required by applicable law.

Employees generally must request planned PTO at least five business days in advance. Manager approval is required.

Northstar does not pay out unused PTO during active employment.

Upon termination of employment, unused PTO is paid only where required by applicable state or local law.

## 3. Sick Time

Full-time employees receive **six paid sick days per calendar year**.

Sick time is separate from PTO.

Unused sick time does not carry over from year to year and is not paid out when employment ends.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents them from working.

## 4. Company Holidays

Northstar observes ten company holidays each calendar year.

In addition, full-time employees receive **two floating holidays** per calendar year.

Floating holidays:

- must be used during the calendar year in which they are granted;
- do not carry forward;
- are separate from PTO;
- require manager approval when scheduled.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Full-time employees may receive:

- up to five paid working days following the death of an immediate family member; or
- up to two paid working days following the death of another qualifying family member.

For this policy, immediate family includes a spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or equivalent step-family relationship.

Bereavement leave does not reduce an employee's PTO balance.

## 6. Parental Leave

Northstar provides paid parental leave to eligible employees.

The eligibility requirements, duration, pay provisions, and rules governing the interaction between parental leave and PTO are contained in the separate **Parental and Family Leave Policy**.

If this handbook conflicts with the Parental and Family Leave Policy, the Parental and Family Leave Policy controls.

## 7. Remote Work

Northstar supports remote and hybrid work where compatible with an employee's position.

Remote-work eligibility, required office attendance, equipment reimbursement, and work-location restrictions are governed by the separate **Remote Work and Information Security Policy**.

Remote work is not an entitlement and may be changed based on business requirements.

## 8. Retirement Benefits

Northstar maintains a tax-qualified 401(k) retirement plan.

This handbook does not define eligibility, employer matching contributions, vesting, loans, withdrawals, or other plan provisions.

Those terms are governed by the current **Northstar Analytics 401(k) Summary Plan Description**.

If information in this handbook conflicts with the Summary Plan Description, the Summary Plan Description controls.

## 9. Tuition Assistance

Full-time employees who have completed at least 12 continuous months of employment may apply for tuition assistance.

Northstar reimburses up to **$5,000 per calendar year** for approved education expenses.

The course must be related to:

- the employee's current position;
- a reasonably anticipated future position at Northstar; or
- a degree or certification program approved by the employee's department head.

An employee must receive a grade of **B or higher**, or a passing grade for a pass/fail course, to receive reimbursement.

Employees who voluntarily resign within six months after receiving tuition reimbursement must repay 50% of the reimbursement received during that six-month period.

## 10. Policy Authority

Northstar may amend its policies prospectively.

Employees should rely on the version marked **Active** with the most recent effective date.

Benefit-plan documents may contain rules different from this handbook. Where applicable, the official benefit-plan document controls.
