# Northstar Analytics 401(k) Plan
## Summary Plan Description

**Plan Year:** January 1–December 31  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Status:** Active

This Summary Plan Description (“SPD”) summarizes key provisions of the Northstar Analytics 401(k) Plan.

In the event of a conflict between this summary and the official Plan Document, the Plan Document controls.

## 1. Employee Eligibility

Full-time and part-time employees become eligible to participate after completing **90 days of service**.

Temporary employees and independent contractors are not eligible to participate.

An eligible employee may begin making elective contributions on the first payroll period administratively practicable following completion of the eligibility requirement.

## 2. Automatic Enrollment

Eligible employees who do not make an affirmative contribution election are automatically enrolled at a pre-tax contribution rate equal to **4% of eligible compensation**.

Employees may change their contribution rate or opt out of automatic enrollment at any time, subject to normal payroll processing deadlines.

## 3. Employee Contributions

Participants may make:

- pre-tax contributions;
- Roth 401(k) contributions; or
- a combination of pre-tax and Roth contributions.

Participant contributions remain subject to applicable federal limits.

Northstar does not determine the federal annual contribution limit in this SPD because the statutory limit may change from year to year.

## 4. Employer Matching Contribution

For eligible employee contributions, Northstar provides the following matching contribution:

**100% of employee contributions on the first 3% of eligible compensation contributed, plus 50% of employee contributions on the next 2% of eligible compensation contributed.**

No matching contribution is provided for employee contributions exceeding 5% of eligible compensation.

Examples:

An employee contributing 2% of eligible compensation receives a 2% employer match.

An employee contributing 3% receives a 3% employer match.

An employee contributing 4% receives a 3.5% employer match.

An employee contributing 5% receives a 4% employer match.

An employee contributing 8% still receives a maximum employer match equal to 4% of eligible compensation.

## 5. Vesting

Employee contributions are always **100% vested**.

Employer matching contributions become vested according to the following schedule:

| Completed Years of Vesting Service | Vested Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Less than 1 year | 0% |
| 1 year | 25% |
| 2 years | 50% |
| 3 years | 75% |
| 4 or more years | 100% |

For purposes of this provision, vesting service is determined according to the terms of the official Plan Document.

## 6. Plan Loans

Participants may generally have no more than **one outstanding plan loan at a time**.

The minimum loan amount is $1,000.

The maximum permitted loan is generally the lesser of:

- $50,000; or
- 50% of the participant's vested account balance,

subject to applicable legal requirements and adjustments required by law.

Loan repayment generally occurs through payroll deduction.

Additional requirements apply and are available through the Plan's recordkeeper.

## 7. Hardship Withdrawals

A participant may request a hardship distribution when the participant satisfies applicable plan and legal requirements for an immediate and heavy financial need.

Approval is not automatic.

Participants should consult the Plan's recordkeeper for current eligibility rules, documentation requirements, tax consequences, and available alternatives.

## 8. Termination of Employment

A participant's vested account balance remains the participant's property upon termination.

Any unvested employer matching contributions are forfeited according to the Plan's forfeiture provisions.

Distribution options depend on account balance, age, Plan rules, and applicable law.

## 9. Relationship to Other Northstar Documents

Information about the 401(k) Plan that appears in an employee handbook, onboarding guide, presentation, email, or other informal communication does not supersede this SPD.

If another Northstar policy provides different information regarding:

- eligibility;
- employer matching;
- vesting;
- withdrawals;
- loans; or
- distributions,

employees should rely on this SPD and, ultimately, the official Plan Document.

## 10. External Legal Limits

Federal contribution limits and certain other retirement-plan thresholds may change periodically.

Questions regarding the **current federal annual contribution limit** should therefore be answered using an authoritative current external source rather than assuming that a historical limit remains valid.
