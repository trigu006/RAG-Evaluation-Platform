# Northstar Analytics, Inc.
## Parental and Family Leave Policy

**Version:** 2.2  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026  
**Status:** Active

This policy governs Northstar's company-provided parental leave benefit.

This policy does not describe every leave entitlement that may exist under federal, state, or local law.

## 1. Eligibility

Full-time employees become eligible for Northstar paid parental leave after completing **six continuous months of employment**.

Part-time employees, temporary employees, and independent contractors are not eligible for company-provided paid parental leave.

## 2. Qualifying Events

Paid parental leave may be used following:

- the birth of an employee's child;
- adoption of a child by the employee; or
- placement of a foster child with the employee.

## 3. Duration

Eligible employees may receive up to **eight weeks of paid parental leave** for a qualifying event.

Parental leave must normally be completed within 12 months following the qualifying event.

## 4. Compensation

Approved parental leave is paid at **100% of the employee's regular base salary** for up to eight weeks.

Bonuses, commissions, overtime, and other variable compensation are not included when calculating parental-leave pay.

## 5. Interaction With PTO

Employees are **not required to use PTO concurrently with Northstar's eight weeks of paid parental leave**.

An employee may request to use accrued PTO immediately before or immediately after paid parental leave, subject to manager and HR approval.

Using PTO does not extend the eight-week company-paid parental-leave benefit itself.

Example:

An employee with two weeks of approved accrued PTO may take eight weeks of paid parental leave followed by two weeks of PTO, resulting in ten consecutive weeks away from work.

## 6. PTO Accrual During Leave

Employees continue to accrue PTO while receiving Northstar-paid parental leave.

PTO does **not** accrue during an unpaid leave of absence lasting more than 30 consecutive calendar days.

## 7. Holidays During Parental Leave

A Northstar company holiday that occurs during an employee's paid parental leave does not extend the total eight-week parental-leave period.

The holiday is considered part of the leave period.

## 8. Multiple Qualifying Events

Paid parental leave is limited to **eight weeks in a rolling 12-month period**, regardless of the number of qualifying events occurring during that period.

## 9. Notice

When the need for leave is foreseeable, employees should provide at least 30 days' notice.

When 30 days' notice is not practicable, notice should be provided as soon as reasonably possible.

## 10. Interaction With Other Leave Laws

An employee may also qualify for leave under federal, state, or local law.

Where legally permitted, Northstar parental leave may run concurrently with other applicable leave entitlements.

Employees should contact Human Resources when their question depends on statutory leave eligibility or jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## 11. Controlling Policy

This policy controls Northstar's company-provided parental-leave benefit.

If the general Employee Handbook contains different information regarding the duration of parental leave, eligibility, compensation, or interaction with PTO, this policy controls.
